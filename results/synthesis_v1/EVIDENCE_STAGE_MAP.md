## Discovery versus confirmation

Phase 1.2 selected O3 CVaR after observing the 56-task discovery set. Phase 2
used 84 held-out tasks on 15 nonoverlapping base graphs under a frozen
preregistration. The stages remain separate. Their rows are not pooled into a
new effect estimate.

## Resource-censored and excluded evidence

the resource guard. That absence is visible as censoring, not encoded as a zero
or scientific failure. Unfinished Phase 3B material in another worktree is

for reproducibility. They are not all direct manuscript evidence. The inventory
column `included_in_manuscript` records this distinction without deleting or
altering any asset.

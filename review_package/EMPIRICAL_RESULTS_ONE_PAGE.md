# Empirical Results (One Page)

- Phase 0 v1: 175 tasks, 53 duplicate feasible-set levels. Phase 0 v2: 140
  tasks, zero duplicate levels, phi from 1.90735e-6 to 0.0234375.
- Scale audit: all 140 controlled Hamiltonians retain the correct ground state;
  raw scale covariation motivated the frozen contract.
- Pilot: 56 tasks/10 graphs/504 rows; original p=3 response was anomalous and
  exploratory.
- Optimization diagnostic: nested identity 168/168; 29/168 original p=3 runs
  underperformed embedded p=2; continuation improved G in 27/29.
- Objective discovery: median O2--O0 capacity gap 0.2193 decades; O3 residual
  gap 0.0069. O2 is a mechanistic exact-feasibility ceiling.
- Phase 2 held-out: 84 tasks/15 nonoverlapping graphs. H1 O3--O0 graph mean
  0.354714949 decades, one-sided lower 0.237378536, Holm p=0.000244141. H2
  O3--O2 -0.00859515 against -0.10 margin, lower -0.03604875, same Holm p.
- Secondary Phase 2: O3/O0 P_opt wins 80/84; both P_feas and P_opt increased on
  79/84 tasks. Tail and energy-separation checks passed 84/84.
- Phase 3: 180-task universe; completed through m=20; m=22 resource-censored.
  O3 reverses relative to O0 at the largest completed size. No global scaling
  law is claimed.

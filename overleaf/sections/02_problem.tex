\section{Problem and Estimands}
\label{sec:problem}

Let $G=(V,E)$ be a directed graph with source $s$, target $t$, positive edge
costs $c_e$, nonnegative resource consumptions $r_e$, and budget $B$. The
single-resource RCSP is
\begin{equation}
  \min_{P}\sum_{e\in P}c_e
  \quad\text{subject to}\quad
  \sum_{e\in P}r_e\leq B,
  \label{eq:rcsp}
\end{equation}
over directed $s$--$t$ paths $P$ \cite{beasley1989rcsp}. The controlled
instances are synthetic layered directed acyclic graphs.

Each edge has a bit $x_e$. With signed incidence coefficients $a_{ve}$ and
$d_s=1,d_t=-1,d_v=0$ otherwise, feasibility requires
\begin{equation}
  \sum_e a_{ve}x_e=d_v\quad(v\in V),
  \qquad \sum_e r_ex_e\leq B.
  \label{eq:constraints}
\end{equation}
Selections that violate flow or resource constraints remain computational-
basis states. If $m=|E|$ and $M_{\rm state}$ strings are feasible, define
\begin{equation}
  \phistate=\frac{M_{\rm state}}{2^m},\qquad
  D=-\log_{10}\phistate,\qquad
  \gfeas(\theta)=\log_{10}\frac{\pfeas(\theta)}{\phistate}.
  \label{eq:dilution}
\end{equation}
Thus $D$ measures dilution in this edge-bit representation and $\gfeas$
measures feasible-mass gain over its uniform starting state. The path-domain
fraction $M/K$ among $K$ candidate routes generally differs from
$\phistate$ because most bit strings are not routes.

The normalized cost Hamiltonian $H_C$ is diagonal. At alternating-layer count
$p$, Penalty-X uses
\begin{equation}
  \lvert\psi_p(\boldsymbol\gamma,\boldsymbol\beta)\rangle
  =\prod_{\ell=1}^{p}e^{-i\beta_\ell\sum_eX_e}
   e^{-i\gamma_\ell H_C}\lvert+\rangle^{\otimes m}.
  \label{eq:qaoa}
\end{equation}
Feasibility is encoded only through $H_C$; neither the initial state nor the
transverse-$X$ mixer preserves it.

The main objective chain applies three classical losses to the same circuit
and $H_C$. O0 is mean energy,
$L_{\rm O0}=\langle H_C\rangle$. O2 is
$L_{\rm O2}=1-\pfeas$ and directly measures the tested statevector ansatz's
feasible-concentration capacity. O3 is exact lower-tail
$\cvar_{0.10}(H_C)$: energy--probability pairs are sorted, the lowest-energy
probability mass 0.10 is averaged, and only the required fraction of the
cutoff mass is included. CVaR is established in variational optimization
\cite{barkoutsos2020cvar}; its use here is evaluated, not claimed as new. A
secondary expected-penalty loss O1 and the complete objective definitions are
reported in Online Resource~1, Sec.~S3.

For feasible strings $\mathcal F$ and exact-optimal strings
$\mathcal O\subseteq\mathcal F$, the operational endpoints are
\begin{equation}
 \pfeas=\sum_{x\in\mathcal F}p_\theta(x),\qquad
 \popt=\sum_{x\in\mathcal O}p_\theta(x),\qquad
 \popt=\pfeas\pcond,
 \label{eq:decomposition}
\end{equation}
where $\pcond=\popt/\pfeas$. This identity separates entry into the feasible
set from selection of an optimum after entry; mean energy is not substituted
for either endpoint.

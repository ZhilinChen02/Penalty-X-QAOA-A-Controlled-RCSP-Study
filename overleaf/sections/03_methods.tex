\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Controlled benchmark and Hamiltonian}

The deterministic generator forms layered directed acyclic graphs with
positive integer costs in $[1,9]$ and resources in $[1,1000]$. Five base
graphs at each target edge count $m\in\{7,10,13,16,19\}$ contribute up to
seven distinct achievable feasible-route cardinalities. Exact route and
edge-bit enumeration records feasible and optimal sets independently; the
resulting 140 tasks contain no duplicate primary feasible sets. Enumeration
is reference computation at these sizes, not a performance baseline.

For an edge string $x$, let
$f_{\rm raw}(x)=\sum_v(\sum_ea_{ve}x_e-d_v)^2$,
$u(x)=\max\{0,\sum_er_ex_e-B\}$,
$S_f=\max_x f_{\rm raw}(x)$, and $S_r=\sum_er_e$. We use
\begin{align}
 P_{\rm flow}(x)&=\ind[f_{\rm raw}>0]+\min\{f_{\rm raw}/S_f,1\},\nonumber\\
 P_{\rm resource}(x)&=\ind[u>0]+\min\{(u/S_r)^2,1\},\nonumber\\
 E_{\rm raw}(x)&=C(x)+172(P_{\rm flow}+P_{\rm resource}),
 &H_C(x)&=E_{\rm raw}(x)/172.
 \label{eq:controlled-energy}
\end{align}
The coefficient 172 is one above the generator-wide routing-cost bound
$19\times9=171$. Every violation therefore lies above every feasible routing
cost. Raw and normalized ground-state sets match the exact RCSP optima on
140/140 tasks; the controlled absolute energy span is 490.51--744.17, and the
median within-base maximum/minimum span ratio is 1.07495.

\subsection{Optimization and matched attribution}

All reported circuit states are exact CPU statevectors. The discovery pilot
runs $p=1,2,3$ from three fixed random seeds. Pilot $\gamma$ coordinates are
initialized in $[0,2\pi]$ and $\beta$ coordinates in $[0,\pi]$; after
initialization the optimizer coordinates are unbounded. The implementation
neither bounds nor reduces $\gamma$ modulo $2\pi$, and we do not assume
fundamental $2\pi$ periodicity for the generally noninteger spectrum of the
normalized Hamiltonian.

COBYLA receives 120 objective evaluations for the pilot and nested diagnostic
and 240 for each matched objective arm. For
$(\gamma_1,\gamma_2,\beta_1,\beta_2)$, the exact embedding
\begin{equation}
 \iota(\theta_2)=(\gamma_1,\gamma_2,0,\beta_1,\beta_2,0)
 \label{eq:embedding}
\end{equation}
reproduces the $p=2$ state inside $p=3$. A deeper terminal objective worse
than this known point is therefore a run-specific certificate of optimizer
inadequacy, not proof of limited ansatz capacity. Passing this necessary test
does not certify global optimality.

All O0/O2/O3 matched arms use one common mean-energy-selected $p=2$ state,
embedded with a zero third layer. The common starting point is deliberate: it
holds initialization fixed so that subsequent differences are attributable to
the classical loss. The design does not estimate performance under objective-
specific initialization policies.

\subsection{Frozen confirmation and robustness}

The discovery set contains 56 tasks from ten base graphs; the held-out set is
its task-and-graph complement, 84 tasks from 15 graphs. CVaR-0.10 was selected
on discovery only. Before held-out execution, H1 (O3--O0 $>0$) and H2
(O3--O2 non-inferiority margin $-0.10$ decades) were frozen with the graph as
analysis unit. Task contrasts are averaged within graph; one-sided 95\%
lower bounds use 10,000 graph bootstrap resamples, exact sign-flip tests use
all $2^{15}$ graph sign assignments, and Holm adjustment covers H1/H2.
Discovery and held-out observations are never pooled for a new inferential
estimate.

Post-hoc protocols were frozen before their formal runs and do not alter H1 or
H2. They cover the nested diagnostic under COBYLA, Nelder--Mead, and SLSQP; an
outcome-blind 24-task $p\in\{2,3,4\}$ subset at 120, 240, and 480 calls; six
discovery CVaR tail fractions; fixed-endpoint sampling; and finite-shot
retraining on ten preselected held-out graphs. An exact label-setting solver
checks classical tractability \cite{beasley1989rcsp}. Scaling uses a separate
prespecified size-conditioned design and a pre-optimization computational-
budget guard. Complete task selection, optimizer options, seed derivations,
stopping rules, statistical details, and result matrices are in Online
Resource~1, Secs.~S2--S8.

Runs retain deterministic identities and all failure or censoring states.
Canonical data and frozen outputs are hash protected. OpenAI Codex assisted
with code, audits, and document preparation; the human author remains
responsible for protocols, results, interpretations, and the submitted text.

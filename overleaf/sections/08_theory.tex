\section{Scope of the Dilution Interpretation}
\label{sec:theory}

Raw edge-bit feasible density is not intrinsic RCSP hardness. An explicit
$m$-edge directed chain has one route and hence $\phistate=2^{-m}$, yet its
solution is read in $O(m)$ time. Subdividing an edge similarly changes the
encoding statistic while preserving route order and optima:
\begin{equation}
 \phistate(I^{(r)})=2^{-(r-1)}\phistate(I).
 \label{eq:padding}
\end{equation}
Thus $\phistate$ is operationally relevant to the tested full-space encoding,
but does not alone imply an explicit-input lower bound; graph-query conclusions
depend on representation and access \cite{durr2006graph,galperin1983succinct}.

A narrower statement applies when the only unknown-set information is a
counted membership query. For a uniformly random size-$M$ marked subset of an
$N$-label domain, a protocol with a pathwise hard cap of $q$ such queries
satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \mathbb E_{\mathcal F}P_{\rm success}
 \leq \min\{1,(2q+1)^2M/N\}.
 \label{eq:membership-bound}
\end{equation}
Training and feedback queries count if they use that interface. This is an
average-case oracle statement, not a pointwise claim for every natural
feasible set. The membership-query arguments adapt standard Grover/BBBV-style
search bounds \cite{bennett1997strengths,boyer1998tight,zalka1999optimal,
hoyer2000arbitrary,brassard2002amplitude} and are used here to delimit the
information-access model rather than as a claim of a new generic quantum-
search lower bound.

The experiment exposes the full multilevel RCSP energy, and CVaR uses its
ordering and weights. The membership-only bound therefore does not lower-bound
the rich-energy O3 experiment. Candidate lists, advice, warm starts, feasible-
state loaders, oracles, and structured mixers also change the accessible
information. Search-with-advice results make this dependence explicit
\cite{montanaro2010advice,he2021prior,nayebi2015inverting,
chung2020advice,konig2009entropy,anderson2024easier}. Such structure can reduce
the search-space burden while relocating cost into preprocessing, information
storage, state preparation, mixer or oracle construction, validation, and
reuse. Full theorem statements, assumptions, boundary cases, proofs,
posterior/advice formulations, and counterexamples are in Online Resource~1,
Sec.~S1.

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Full-space Penalty-X QAOA starts from the uniform state over all $2^m$
edge-bit strings and uses a transverse-$X$ mixer that does not preserve
feasibility. In constrained routing, feasible routes may occupy only a minute
part of that representation. Poor feasible sampling can then reflect several
different mechanisms: representation-induced dilution, an uncontrolled
penalty scale, classical optimizer inadequacy, a training loss that is
misaligned with feasible entry, or insufficient ansatz capacity. An endpoint
probability alone does not identify which mechanism is responsible.

We separate these explanations on a controlled resource-constrained shortest-
path (RCSP) benchmark. The primary study uses exact CPU statevectors, a uniform
$\lvert+\rangle$ initial state, a diagonal scale-controlled penalty
Hamiltonian, the transverse-$X$ mixer, and alternating-layer count $p\leq3$;
a post-hoc robustness subset reaches $p=4$. Here ``shallow'' refers to $p$,
not compiled hardware depth. The benchmark has 140 distinct-cardinality tasks
on 25 synthetic layered directed acyclic graphs and spans raw edge-bit
feasible fractions from $1.91\times10^{-6}$ to $2.34\times10^{-2}$.

The central comparison is the sequence O0$\rightarrow$O2$\rightarrow$O3. O0
minimizes mean Hamiltonian energy. O2 directly maximizes exact feasible mass
and is used only as a mechanistic statevector capacity control: O2--O0 asks
whether the same ansatz can reach feasibility that O0 does not induce. O3
minimizes lower-tail CVaR without changing the quantum Hamiltonian or ansatz;
O3--O2 asks whether an energy-based loss approaches that measured capacity.
A graph-disjoint discovery/held-out design freezes CVaR-0.10 before the two
held-out tests, including H2 non-inferiority to O2. O2 is not proposed as a
deployable objective.

Exact ansatz nesting provides a separate optimizer diagnostic. Adding a
zero-angle layer embeds every $p=2$ state in the $p=3$ family, so a worse
reported deeper objective certifies optimizer inadequacy for that run. This
certificate does not establish that passing runs are global optima, nor does
it make objective alignment automatic. Post-hoc checks extend the diagnostic
across COBYLA, Nelder--Mead, SLSQP, $p=4$, tested evaluation budgets, CVaR tail
fractions, and finite-shot estimation and training. They qualify the frozen
study rather than enlarge its confirmatory family.

The paper makes four contributions:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(C\arabic*)]
  \item a controlled RCSP dilution benchmark and Hamiltonian scale contract
        that preserve every exact ground state;
  \item nested capacity/optimizer diagnosis, with robustness across tested
        optimizers and depth;
  \item objective attribution through the O0--O2--O3 sequence and frozen
        held-out confirmation of H1 and H2; and
  \item explicit robustness boundaries for tail fraction, finite shots,
        depth--budget interaction, high-size reversal, and classical
        tractability.
\end{enumerate}

The exact label-setting solver reproduces all 140 frozen optima, so the tasks
are classically tractable mechanism probes, not evidence of quantum advantage.

theorem statements and proofs, and reproducibility provenance.

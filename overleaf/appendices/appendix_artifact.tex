\subsection{Frozen evidence boundary}

Finalization began from baseline source commit
\texttt{4d1111f3661f}; its complete SHA is retained in the artifact manifest.
Canonical evidence roots are
the project-relative directories
\path{results/phase0/},
\path{results/phase0_v2_dilution_stress/},
\path{results/phase1_pilot_v1/},
\path{results/phase1_1_optimization_diagnostic/},
\path{results/phase1_2_objective_alignment/},
\path{results/phase2_confirmatory_v1/},
\path{results/phase3_scaling_v1/}, and
\path{results/theory_validation_v1/} through
\path{results/theory_validation_v3/}. No unfinished Phase 3B path supplies a
scientific value, claim, figure, or table.

The ten base manuscript figures (main-article Figs.~1--4 and Online
Resource~1, Figs.~S7--S12) are
regenerated from canonical CSV/JSON rows by
\path{paper_scripts/build_paper_assets.py}. That presentation script
performs deterministic filtering and aggregation only; it does not run QAOA,
modify source evidence, or refit a held-out analysis. Online Resource~1,
Fig.~S1, is generated separately by
\path{paper_scripts/run_posthoc_finite_shot_endpoint.py} from the
fixed held-out O0/O3 endpoints and is labeled post-hoc robustness only. Raw
statevectors and individual samples are not persisted. The figure and table
manifests record input files, filters, aggregations, rounding, and output
names.

Reviewer-robustness Figs.~S2--S6 of this Online Resource and the associated
tables are copied or rendered from the isolated
\path{results/reviewer_robustness/} namespace by
\path{paper_scripts/reviewer_robustness/build_revision_assets.py}.
They are explicitly post hoc and do not modify the canonical directories or
the frozen confirmatory family. A separate manuscript-number audit recomputes
every new displayed headline from its formal CSV source and verifies its
presence in the assembled manuscript.

\subsection{Compact public provenance}

The source package is self-contained for compilation but intentionally does
not copy the large scientific result tree. The compact machine-readable files
described below are distributed together as Online Resource~2.
\path{claim_evidence_summary.csv} maps each candidate claim
to its project-relative canonical source, scope, allowed wording, and
prohibited wording. \path{numeric_audit.csv} provides the
frozen synthesis-level numeric audit. The full retained execution census and
the task-stratum summary are supplied as
\path{full_failure_census.csv} and
\path{task_strata.csv}. The package-level
\path{artifact_manifest.txt} records SHA-256 values for
included files and selected source artifacts.

\subsection{Validation targets}

The artifact checks enforce that all predecessor hashes remain unchanged,
every manuscript claim has a claim-matrix source, headline numbers map to
canonical evidence, unfinished Phase 3B paths are absent, citation keys
resolve, figure/table inputs exist, and theorem dependencies are closed. The
project-wide test suite is run unchanged after paper assembly. Static brace,
environment, input, label/reference, citation, and missing-asset checks are
supplemented by a clean temporary-directory \LaTeX{} build when a compiler is
available; build logs and the resulting PDF are retained outside the frozen
science directories.

\subsection{Human confirmation and external validation}

The following are deliberately unresolved in this package: author
affiliation, acknowledgments, funding, and repository/DOI URL. Independent
human proof review should prioritize the adaptive and
posterior arguments. Independent prior-art review should trace citations around
multiple-marked search, arbitrary phases, search with advice, and
preprocessing. Independent external reproduction remains pending. A separate
reproducer should rebuild the Phase-2 graph-level contrasts and multiplicity
adjustment from canonical rows and confirm Phase-3 censoring before any
submission claim is finalized.

\subsection{Energy-tail checks}

For each task, the maximum energy over all valid feasible states was compared
with the minimum energy over all other states. Strict separation held on
140/140 v2 tasks. The O3 tail check compares the Boolean event
$\pfeas\geq\alpha$ with whether every probability contribution to the exact
lowest-energy $\alpha$ tail is feasible, including the fractional cutoff mass.
It held on 56/56 discovery and 84/84 held-out O3 cells.

\section{Held-Out Preregistration and Statistics}
\label{app:statistics}

\subsection{Frozen hypotheses and analysis unit}

For graph $g$, let $L_g$ denote its full set of planned dilution levels and
define
\begin{align}
 \Delta_{1g}
 &=|L_g|^{-1}\sum_{\ell\in L_g}
 [\gfeas(\mathrm{O3};g,\ell)-\gfeas(\mathrm{O0};g,\ell)],\\
 \Delta_{2g}
 &=|L_g|^{-1}\sum_{\ell\in L_g}
 [\gfeas(\mathrm{O3};g,\ell)-\gfeas(\mathrm{O2};g,\ell)].
\end{align}
All 15 graphs were complete. The population estimands are arithmetic means of
$\Delta_{1g}$ and $\Delta_{2g}$ over graph units. H1 has null margin zero. H2
has non-inferiority margin $-0.10$ decades and is tested after shifting
$\Delta_{2g}$ by $+0.10$.

The exact randomization-style $p$-value enumerates all sign vectors
$s\in\{-1,+1\}^{15}$ and compares
$15^{-1}\sum_gs_g\Delta_g$ with the observed mean in the one-sided direction.
The two resulting values enter Holm's two-hypothesis step-down correction.
The grouped bootstrap samples 15 graph identifiers with replacement 10,000
times under seed 20261017 and takes the fifth percentile of the bootstrap mean
as its one-sided lower bound. Individual task rows are never resampled as
independent units.

The H1 estimate is 0.3547149490180106, with bootstrap standard error

multiplicity family.

Secondary task-level results include 80 O3-over-O0 $\popt$ wins, four losses,
and no ties; 79 tasks improve both $\pfeas$ and $\popt$. O3 ends at a higher

but do not enlarge its hypothesis family.

\section{Post-Hoc Finite-Shot Endpoint Robustness}
\label{app:finite-shot}

\subsection{Status and Design}
This analysis is labeled
\texttt{POSTHOC\_FINITE\_SHOT\_ENDPOINT\_ROBUSTNESS} and has the
scientific role \texttt{POSTHOC\_ROBUSTNESS\_ONLY}. It samples the frozen
O0 and O3 $p=3$ terminal distributions for all 84 held-out tasks; it does not
rerun optimization, change terminal parameters, or enter the preregistered
H1/H2 family. The 168 endpoint distributions were regenerated
deterministically from the task generator, penalty contract, simulator
convention, and stored terminal angles. The maximum absolute difference from
the authoritative exact endpoint metrics was
$7.33\times10^{-15}$.

Each endpoint was sampled at 1,000, 10,000, and 100,000 shots with 20
deterministic replicates. A seed is the SHA-256-derived identity of task,
objective, shot count, and replicate. Every replicate estimates $\pfeas$,
$\popt$, mean normalized energy, and empirical lowest-energy CVaR-0.10.
The CVaR implementation accumulates probability mass in ascending energy
order and fractionally weights the cutoff observation when
$\alpha N_{\mathrm{shots}}$ is noninteger; for the three reported shot
counts the cutoff is integral.

\subsection{Estimator Summary}
For O0/O3, respectively, CVaR mean absolute error decreases from
0.0275/0.0352 at 1,000 shots to 0.0027/0.0034 at 100,000 shots.
The corresponding $\pfeas$ mean absolute errors decrease from
0.0041/0.0056 to 0.00043/0.00054. Across task--replicate comparisons, the
sampled sign of the O3-versus-O0 $\pfeas$ contrast agrees with the exact
sign in 94.8\%, 99.3\%, and 100.0\% of cases at 1,000, 10,000, and
100,000 shots. Full bias, standard deviation, RMSE, percentile, relative-
error, and ordering summaries are retained in the post-hoc result directory.
Ordering recovery is conditioned on a non-tied exact comparison, and exact
ties are reported separately.

# Venue Strategy

The venue comparison uses official scope and author-policy pages current at the
2026-08-29 audit. Journal metrics are not used. Policies and fees must be
rechecked immediately before submission.

## Primary fit: INFORMS Journal on Computing

The empirical-primary manuscript is closest to an operations-research
computational study: RCSP is central, the design isolates optimization and
objective mechanisms, the held-out analysis is frozen, and the artifact is
substantial. The official editorial statement asks quantum-computing work to
connect to operations research, while the submission guidance emphasizes
computational reproducibility and currently gives a 25-page main limit with
documented appendix allowances. The manuscript must therefore foreground the
OR question and make the controlled synthetic benchmark scientifically useful,
not present QAOA as a generic physics result.

Official sources:

- <https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/ijoc/editorial-statement>
- <https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/ijoc/submission-guidelines>

## Strong secondary fits

**Quantum Science and Technology** covers theoretical and experimental quantum
science and expects a significant advance. The controlled empirical mechanism
and artifact fit, but lack of hardware and a known search-theory core raise the
significance burden. It is a hybrid open-access journal according to the
official publisher page.

- <https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/journals/quantum-science-technology/about-quantum-science-technology/>

**Physical Review A** is plausible for a technically scoped numerical and
analytical paper. Regular Articles have flexible length in current APS author
guidance; Letters are too compressed for the evidence chain. Hardware is not a
categorical requirement, but an RCSP-only empirical narrative may be viewed as
narrow.

- <https://journals.aps.org/pra/authors>

**Quantum** is a plausible open-access venue if the manuscript demonstrates a
general methodological lesson from the RCSP evidence and gives a rigorously
reproducible artifact. Its significance threshold and current author policies
must be rechecked; unresolved theory priority argues against a theory-led pitch.

- <https://quantum-journal.org/about/>

## Possible but weaker fits

**ACM Transactions on Quantum Computing** spans theory and practice and could
fit an artifact-led quantum-algorithms study. A theory-primary submission would
need a clearer contribution beyond known search/advice results.

- <https://acm-stoc.org/stoc2020/acm-journals/tqc-new-announcement-06-2020.pdf>

**Quantum Information Processing** has broad algorithmic scope and accepts
theory/computational work. Its breadth lowers topic mismatch but does not remove
the need for conservative novelty positioning.

- <https://link.springer.com/journal/11128/aims-and-scope>

**IEEE Transactions on Quantum Engineering** emphasizes engineering systems and
applications and is fully open access. The absence of hardware, compilation,
and deployment-resource evidence makes the present package less engineering-
complete.

- <https://tqe.ieee.org/>

## High-risk or future options

**PRX Quantum** seeks exceptional advances, connections, capabilities, or
insights and is fully open access. The controlled RCSP case study plus unresolved
theory priority does not currently meet a defensible PRX-style pitch.

- <https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/scope>
- <https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/about>

**IEEE QCE** could support a shorter future algorithms/applications paper, but
the official 2026 full-paper call used an 8--10 page format and its deadline had
already passed by this audit date. It is not an immediate submission target.

- <https://qce.quantum.ieee.org/2026/call-for-technical-papers/>

## Submission-order recommendation

Prepare the empirical-primary structured draft for an OR/computing audience,
but seek external theorem and prior-art review before prose freeze. Re-evaluate
INFORMS Journal on Computing and Quantum Science and Technology using the draft's
actual balance. Do not select a theory-centric venue until contribution priority
is independently reviewed.

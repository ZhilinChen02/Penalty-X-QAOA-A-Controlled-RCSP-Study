\section*{Adaptive hard-cap feasible-space dilution theorem}

\tagclaim{V1\_THEOREM} The fixed-query theorem is unchanged. Let $\F$ be
uniform over size-$M$ subsets of an $N$-label basis and let $\phi=M/N$.

\subsection*{Model}
\tagclaim{V2\_NEW\_PROOF} An algorithm begins in an $\F$-independent state,
may use arbitrary ancillas, intermediate measurements, discarded environments,
classical randomness and feed-forward, and makes at most $q$ membership-oracle
calls on every branch. No other input or operation depends on $\F$. It outputs
a label $x$ and succeeds iff $x\in\F$.

\subsection*{Hard-cap theorem}
\tagclaim{V2\_NEW\_PROOF} For the phase-flip oracle
$O_\F=I-2\Pi_\F$,
\[
 \mathbb E_\F P_\F\le \min\{1,(2q+1)^2\phi\}.
\]
The expectation also averages internal measurement and random outcomes.

\subsection*{Proof}
\tagclaim{V2\_NEW\_PROOF} Introduce query, work, transcript, random-seed,
environment, halt, scratch, and output registers. Replace every instrument by
its Stinespring isometry, retaining its outcome and discarded data in
orthogonal transcript/environment registers. Purify random choices and make
feed-forward coherently controlled. Pad to $q$ slots by controlled-swapping an
isolated scratch query register into the oracle port on halted branches,
applying the ordinary oracle unconditionally, and swapping back. Halted scratch
data never recouple to the output. Reversibly copy the selected classical output
label into the final query register.

The resulting pure algorithm has $q$ oracle uses and $\F$-independent
inter-query unitaries. In the identity-oracle reference algorithm,
\[
 \mathbb E_\F\|(\Pi_\F\otimes I)|\varphi_t\rangle\|^2=\phi.
\]
Since
\[
 \|(O_\F-I)|\varphi_t\rangle\|
 =2\|(\Pi_\F\otimes I)|\varphi_t\rangle\|,
\]
the hybrid recursion and Minkowski imply
$(\mathbb E d_q^2)^{1/2}\le2q\sqrt\phi$. The reference output has average
marked mass $\phi$, so a second projection/Minkowski step gives
$\sqrt{\mathbb E P_\F}\le(2q+1)\sqrt\phi$. Squaring and capping at one proves
the result.

\subsection*{Membership-bit corollary}
\tagclaim{V2\_NEW\_PROOF} For
$B_\F|x,b\rangle=|x,b\oplus1[x\in\F]\rangle$,
\[
 B_\F-I=\Pi_\F\otimes(X-I),\qquad\|X-I\|=2,
\]
so the same coarse theorem holds. Preparing $|-\rangle$ converts one bit query to a
phase flip. The reverse direction requires a controlled phase interface and is
not used in the proof.

\subsection*{History-dependent phases}
\tagclaim{V2\_NEW\_PROOF} If slot $t$ applies the transcript-controlled direct
sum of phases $\gamma_t(h)$, set
\[
 c_t=\sup_h|e^{-i\gamma_t(h)}-1|.
\]
The controlled multiplier has operator norm $c_t$, and hence
\[
 \mathbb E_\F P_\F\le
 \min\left\{1,\left(1+\sum_{t=1}^q c_t\right)^2\phi\right\}.
\]
This assumes the controlled direct sum is the counted oracle interface.

\subsection*{Trained total-query corollary}
\tagclaim{V2\_NEW\_PROOF} A variational controller satisfying the same
oracle-only condition and a deterministic total cap $q_{\rm total}$ obeys
\[
 \mathbb E_\F P_{\rm success}
 \le\min\{1,(2q_{\rm total}+1)^2\phi\}.
\]
Every training circuit, shot, validation call and final execution is counted.
The result does not lower-bound final trained depth alone.

\subsection*{Expected-query caveat}
\tagclaim{COUNTEREXAMPLE} Substituting $\mathbb E Q$ for a hard cap is invalid.
\tagclaim{V2\_NEW\_PROOF} If $\mathbb E Q\le\bar q$, truncation gives, for every
integer $T\ge0$,
\[
 \mathbb E P\le\min\left\{1,\frac{\bar q}{T+1}
 +\min\{1,(2T+1)^2\phi\}\right\}.
\]
If $Q$ is sampled independently before the interaction, conditioning on
$Q=k$ also gives
\[
 \mathbb E P\le
 \mathbb E\!\left[\min\{1,(2Q+1)^2\phi\}\right]
 \le\min\{1,\phi\,\mathbb E(2Q+1)^2\}.
\]
This expected-square statement is not asserted for transcript-dependent
stopping.

\subsection*{Nonclaims}
\tagclaim{OPEN\_GAP} Rich value oracles and standard explicit-input RCSP are
not covered. Free instance-dependent coefficients, feasible-state preparation,
or feasible-subspace mixers violate the oracle-only assumption. No claim is
made that all QAOA requires $\Omega(\phi^{-1/2})$ final circuit depth.
